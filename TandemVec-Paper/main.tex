\textbf{摘要：}
本文研究一种由纵列双电机、正交单轴摆座和反向旋转螺旋桨构成的矢量推力飞行器。
针对理论模型坐标、飞控传感器坐标和悬停操纵语义容易混用的问题，本文首先给出三者之间的
轴置换关系；随后从桨盘动量理论、推力与反扭矩相似律出发，建立执行器到机体六维力/力矩的
非线性映射，并将其嵌入采用四元数运动学的六自由度刚体模型。在控制层，本文以误差四元数
外环、角速度PI内环、惯量与陀螺项前馈以及工作点相关效能矩阵分配组成的绝对指令架构为
主方法。该架构属于非线性动态逆式控制，而非依赖实测角加速度的增量非线性动态逆。
现有证据表明，模型能够复现28\,m/s局部配平、开环慢发散模态和执行器交叉耦合机理；
宿主机回归测试与初步飞行试验则分别约束数值实现和执行器方向。需要强调的是，仿真采用的
MODEL-DEFAULT参数尚未完成台架标定，仿真摆角限幅为$\pm25^\circ$，而固件限幅为
$\pm15^\circ$；因此本文的定量结果用于概念验证，不构成全包线性能或转捩能力证明。

\vspace{0.6em}
\noindent\textbf{关键词：}纵列双发；推力矢量；六自由度动力学；控制分配；误差四元数；非线性动态逆

\vspace{1.2em}
\textbf{Abstract:}
This study investigates a tandem twin-motor vector-thrust aircraft equipped with two orthogonal
single-axis gimbals and counter-rotating propellers. A frame transformation is first established to
separate the analytical propulsion frame, the flight-control sensor frame, and the hover-control
semantics. A nonlinear six-component force and moment map is then derived from actuator-disk
momentum theory and propeller similarity laws and embedded in a six-degree-of-freedom rigid-body
model with quaternion kinematics. The primary controller combines a quaternion-error attitude loop,
a PI body-rate loop, inertia and gyroscopic feedforward, and an operating-point-dependent control-
effectiveness allocation. The implemented controller is an absolute-command nonlinear-dynamic-
inversion-type architecture rather than incremental nonlinear dynamic inversion, because it does not
use measured angular acceleration or accumulate actuator increments. The current evidence supports
local trim, modal, coupling-mechanism, software-regression, and actuator-direction claims. Quantitative
performance remains conceptual: the MODEL-DEFAULT parameters are not fully identified, and the
simulation and firmware use different gimbal limits of $\pm25^\circ$ and $\pm15^\circ$, respectively.

\vspace{0.6em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} tandem propulsion; thrust vectoring; six-degree-of-freedom dynamics;
control allocation; quaternion error; nonlinear dynamic inversion
